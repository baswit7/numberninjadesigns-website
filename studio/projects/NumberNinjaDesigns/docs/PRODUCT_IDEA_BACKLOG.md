# Product Idea Backlog - NumberNinjaDesigns

## Purpose

This backlog turns the NumberNinjaDesigns positioning into a practical queue of product concepts for manual Etsy listing preparation.

Brand constraints:

- dark tactical premium
- data, Excel, analytics, programming and AI audience
- witty, direct and conversion-focused
- office-nerd humor without generic novelty-shirt filler
- manual listing preparation only

## Scoring Method

Confidence is scored from 1 to 10.

Higher scores mean:

- the audience is easy to recognize
- the joke is specific to the work culture
- Etsy keyword intent is clear
- the idea can move through `ETSY_LISTING_WORKFLOW.md` with limited redesign
- trademark and claim risk appear low

## Manual Review Guardrails

Before an idea becomes listing-ready:

- check trademark-sensitive software names before final title and tags
- confirm the design text is readable on mobile mockups
- remove jokes that need too much explanation
- keep the tone tactical, premium and direct
- avoid generic slogans that could fit any tech shirt brand
- publish nothing automatically

## Boundary

- API calls
