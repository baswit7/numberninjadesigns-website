## 1. Recommended Hero Artwork Prompt

Use this prompt first:

```text
Create a print-ready transparent-background t-shirt front artwork for the phrase "PIVOT TABLE OPERATOR".

Style: premium dark tactical tech, analyst culture, spreadsheet command badge, clean technical typography, high contrast, disciplined layout.

Composition: centered vertical badge design, large word "OPERATOR", smaller line "PIVOT TABLE" above it, microcopy "SPREADSHEET RECON UNIT", tiny labels "ROWS", "COLUMNS", "VALUES", "FILTERS", subtle spreadsheet grid and thin technical border.

Colors: off-white main text, muted gray linework, very small neon green accent, designed for black and charcoal shirts.

Production constraints: transparent background, vector-like edges, DTG friendly, no gradients that require photographic printing, no official software logos, no trademarks, no copyrighted characters, no clutter, main phrase readable at thumbnail size.
```

Why this prompt first:

- It matches the highest-confidence `Spreadsheet Recon Badge` direction.
- It is the cleanest bridge between shirt artwork, Etsy thumbnail and social visuals.
- It asks for transparent background and DTG-friendly composition.
- It gives enough visual specificity without overloading the artwork.

## Go/No-Go Review

### Go

Proceed to manual Etsy publication if:

- main artwork reads clearly within two seconds
- `OPERATOR` is the dominant word
- mockup looks premium on a black shirt
- no grid or microcopy clutter hurts readability
- no official logos, trademarks or copyrighted characters are visible
- first five Etsy images look distinct and useful
- title and tags match the actual product
- materials, size, color and shipping fields are verified manually

### No-Go

Do not publish if:

- artwork looks like generic Excel clipart
- microcopy is unreadable or noisy
- mockups hide or distort the shirt design
- any official software logo or trademark-like asset appears
- listing title becomes keyword-stuffed or confusing
- materials or product details are guessed instead of verified
- any publication step depends on automation or unreviewed credentials

## Risk Notes

- `pivot table` is commercially specific but may have narrower reach than broad Excel humor.
- Broad Excel keywords can be crowded; the thumbnail must make the operator angle obvious.
- Over-designed tactical detail could reduce readability and conversion.
- Materials cannot be finalized until the actual Etsy product/provider selection is known.
- Size and shipping notes must come from Etsy/provider data during manual listing entry.
- `Power BI` should remain optional audience language only; do not use official logos or imply affiliation.
