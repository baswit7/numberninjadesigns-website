Use all 10 Etsy image slots to move a buyer from search-result curiosity to purchase confidence.

- lead with clarity
- prove design detail
- show lifestyle fit
- support gift buying
- reduce uncertainty about size, color and use

## Image Quality Checklist

- [ ] Main phrase readable on mobile.
- [ ] No official software logos.
- [ ] No copyrighted characters.
- [ ] No misleading product context.
- [ ] No real confidential data in props.
- [ ] Shirt color and print contrast are clear.
- [ ] Image 1 works without reading the listing title.
- [ ] Images 2 to 10 each add distinct buyer confidence.
