# Mockup Strategy - Pivot Table Operator

## Goal

Create a practical mockup sequence that helps Etsy buyers understand the design quickly, trust the product, and imagine gifting or wearing it.

The mockups must support:

- final shirt artwork review
- Etsy listing conversion
- social media visuals
- gift positioning
- mobile readability

## Ranked Mockups

### 1. Primary Mockup

Purpose:

Show the shirt clearly as the main Etsy thumbnail.

Recommended composition:

- black crewneck shirt
- front-facing or clean flat-lay
- centered `PIVOT TABLE OPERATOR` artwork
- neutral dark or light-gray background
- no props competing with the shirt

Why rank #1:

- highest buyer clarity
- strongest search-result visibility
- safest for mobile thumbnail conversion

Success criteria:

- main phrase readable at thumbnail size
- shirt color and design contrast are obvious
- no extra text outside the shirt is needed

### 2. Close-Up Detail Mockup

Purpose:

Prove print readability and premium detail.

Recommended composition:

- cropped chest area
- visible typography texture
- small green accent visible
- subtle fabric texture

Why rank #2:

- reassures buyer that the artwork is not cluttered
- shows quality and design specificity
- supports premium positioning

Success criteria:

- `OPERATOR` is readable
- microcopy appears intentional, not noisy
- grid detail does not overpower main text

### 3. Desk/Workstation Mockup

Purpose:

Connect the shirt to analyst and spreadsheet culture.

Recommended composition:

- shirt folded or laid near a keyboard, notebook and coffee
- dark desk surface
- abstract spreadsheet-like papers with no real data
- subtle green accent in the scene

Why rank #3:

- reinforces the audience
- useful for Etsy gallery and Pinterest
- gives the product workplace context

Success criteria:

- no official software UI or logos
- product remains central
- scene feels premium, not stock-generic

### 4. Lifestyle Mockup

Purpose:

Show wearability and scale.

Recommended composition:

- model torso only or headless crop
- casual office or work-from-home context
- black or charcoal shirt
- relaxed posture
- design clearly visible

Why rank #4:

- helps buyer judge fit and everyday use
- supports gift purchasing
- shows the design as wearable, not just a graphic

Success criteria:

- no distracting face or background
- print not wrinkled beyond readability
- design remains the focus

### 5. Office Mockup

Purpose:

Position the product as a coworker or team gift.

Recommended composition:

- shirt on chair, desk or hanger in office setting
- clean meeting-room or analyst workspace context
- optional small overlay: `Gift for the spreadsheet operator`

Why rank #5:

- supports gift and team-buying use cases
- less important than the primary product clarity images

Success criteria:

- avoids corporate stock-photo feel
- keeps tone witty and direct
- no visible real company logos or confidential documents

## Mockup Rules

- Use black shirt first.
- Keep design readable in every mockup.
- Avoid official software logos, dashboards or screenshots.
- Avoid fake data that looks confidential.
- Avoid overly dark mockups that hide shirt edges.
- Do not imply automated Etsy, TikTok, Pinterest or Instagram publishing.

## Boundary

This mockup strategy is planning only.

It does not add:

- Etsy automation
- AI image generation
- provider calls
- deployment execution
- credential handling
- secret storage
