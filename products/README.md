# NumberNinjaDesigns products

Every sellable product belongs to exactly one division:

- `digital/`: downloadable files, templates, workbooks, documents, software-assisted packages and their source briefs.
- `physical/`: apparel, print-on-demand products, physical artwork, mockups and licensed-media preparation.

Shared brand rules, market intelligence, APIs, content and automation belong to Studio OS and may support both divisions without creating another public identity.
