What the approved package contains

The approved launch package contains an XLSX workbook and a PDF guide inside a ZIP archive. No customer file is exposed from this preview.

  • Balance and APR overview
  • Minimum plus extra payment planning
  • Principal and interest payment log
  • Payoff progress dashboard
  • Payment sustainability checks

02

A deliberate four-step flow

  1. List

    Enter each balance, APR and planned payment.

  2. Plan

    Set minimum and extra payment assumptions.

  3. Log

    Record principal and interest from completed payments.

  4. Verify

    Review progress and sustainability checks before decisions.

03

Compatibility

  • Microsoft Excel 2021 or later on Windows is the current verified baseline.
  • Google Sheets and other spreadsheet apps are not certified.
  • The interface shown here is a code-native structural preview.

Privacy & offline use

  • This page does not collect or transmit debt information.
  • The intended workbook workflow keeps personal entries in the local file.
  • Future access and delivery mechanics remain outside this launch preview.

Important limits

  • Owner approval is recorded; checkout and live-provider activation remain separate.
  • Results depend on accurate balances, APR and payment entries.
  • This is an organizational and educational tool, not financial, tax, legal, credit or debt-management advice.
