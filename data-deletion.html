Data Deletion

Effective Date: June 26, 2026

NumberNinjaDesigns accepts deletion requests for contact records and data handled through authorized brand workflows.

How to Request Deletion

Email privacy@ninjanumbertees.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and describe the data, platform or communication involved.

Processing

Requests are reviewed based on the information available to NumberNinjaDesigns. If the relevant data cannot be identified, NumberNinjaDesigns may request clarification and identity verification limited to what is necessary for the request.

Etsy Connection

When the Etsy integration is disconnected or authorization is withdrawn, NumberNinjaDesigns deletes the stored OAuth tokens. A deletion request can also cover cached Etsy shop or listing metadata controlled by NumberNinjaDesigns. Etsy account data held by Etsy must be managed through Etsy's own controls.

External Platforms

Pinterest, Meta, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Google, Etsy and Printify manage their own user data, account controls and deletion workflows.

Privacy Contact

Deletion and privacy questions use the same official contact address: privacy@ninjanumbertees.com.
