Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Country of Operation: Netherlands

NumberNinjaDesigns respects privacy and operates this public website with limited data collection, transparent contact handling and official platform API usage only.

Contact Information

Privacy requests can be sent to the NumberNinjaDesigns privacy team. This address is used for privacy and data protection questions.

GDPR Statement

For individuals in the European Economic Area, NumberNinjaDesigns supports applicable GDPR rights, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection where legally available.

Information Collection

The Etsy Promo Publisher creates a pseudonymous secure session and, after authorization, processes the connected Etsy shop identity and active listing metadata, current TikTok creator controls, publication choices, consent evidence, provider status and limited security audit events. OAuth tokens are encrypted server-side. Platform passwords are never collected. With explicit permission, the separate Pinterest tag may process permitted measurement data.

Use of Information

Publisher data is used only to prove Etsy listing ownership, create the requested local preview, apply current TikTok controls, prevent duplicate publication, perform the user-approved upload, provide status readback, secure the service and honor deletion requests. It is not sold.

Third Party Services

NumberNinjaDesigns may use official services from Pinterest, Meta, Instagram, TikTok, Google, Reddit, Printify and Etsy. The optional Pinterest measurement tag loads only after consent. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and account controls.

Data Retention

Publisher sessions expire after 30 days; listing and creator caches expire after 15 minutes and 5 minutes. Completed publication jobs and limited audit events are removed after 90 days. An inactive publisher workspace without a valid session is removed after 31 days. The generated video is not stored by NumberNinjaDesigns: it remains in the visitor's browser recovery storage until publisher data or browser data is cleared. Contact records follow the period reasonably required for the request or law.

Data Security

Publisher sessions use secure HttpOnly cookies and same-origin request protection. OAuth state is unique and single-use; Etsy authorization uses PKCE. Provider tokens are encrypted and tenant-separated server-side. The exact uploaded video is verified against the user's reviewed SHA-256 hash. No public feature asks for a platform password.

Children's Privacy

This website and the Etsy Promo Publisher are not directed to children and do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

International Transfers

Because external platforms may operate internationally, data handled through official APIs may be processed according to those providers' regional infrastructure and policies.

User Rights

Publisher users can disconnect Etsy or TikTok and permanently delete their publisher workspace directly from Connections & data. Users may also request access, correction or deletion by email. External platform controls remain available through each provider.

Cookies and Pinterest Measurement

NumberNinjaDesigns uses the necessary browser-storage key nnd_privacy_consent_v1 to remember whether a visitor accepted or rejected optional Pinterest measurement. This preference does not contain a name, email address or account identifier.

If a visitor accepts Pinterest measurement, the website loads Pinterest tag 2613073368837 and sends a permitted PageVisit event. Pinterest may then set or access measurement identifiers and cookies described in its own documentation. Automatic enhanced matching is disabled: NumberNinjaDesigns does not configure the tag to collect email addresses, names, telephone numbers, dates of birth, addresses or external customer IDs.

Consent can be withdrawn at any time by selecting Privacy choices at the bottom of any public page and turning Pinterest measurement off. The page reloads after withdrawal to stop the active tag, and the website attempts to remove Pinterest cookies accessible from this domain. Cookies controlled by Pinterest must be managed through the visitor's browser or Pinterest privacy settings.

For more information, review the Pinterest Privacy Policy and Pinterest personalization controls. Visitors may also use industry opt-out tools such as AdChoices.

Contact for Privacy Requests

Email the NumberNinjaDesigns privacy team with a clear description of the request and the platform or communication involved.
