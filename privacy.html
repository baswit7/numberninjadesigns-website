Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Country of Operation: Netherlands

NumberNinjaDesigns respects privacy and operates this public website with limited data collection, transparent contact handling and official platform API usage only.

Contact Information

Privacy requests can be sent to privacy@ninjanumbertees.com. This address is used for privacy and data protection questions.

GDPR Statement

For individuals in the European Economic Area, NumberNinjaDesigns supports applicable GDPR rights, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection where legally available.

Information Collection

The website does not offer account registration. Information may be collected when a visitor sends an email, when an official platform API provides data for an account explicitly authorized by the NumberNinjaDesigns owner, or when a visitor explicitly permits marketing measurement.

Use of Information

Information is used to respond to inquiries, process privacy requests, support API reviews, manage authorized brand content workflows and maintain accurate product or listing operations.

Third Party Services

NumberNinjaDesigns may use official services from Pinterest, Meta, Instagram, TikTok, Google, Reddit, Printify and Etsy. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and account controls.

Pinterest Measurement

The Pinterest tag is disabled by default. After explicit marketing consent, Pinterest Tag ID 2613073368837 may record page visits and later supported commerce events for attribution and performance measurement. No enhanced-match email or customer identifier is sent by the current website implementation.

Consent Choice

Your choice is stored locally in this browser. Necessary storage remains active; Pinterest measurement loads only after marketing consent. You can change your choice at any time.

Data Retention

Contact emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed for the request, record keeping or legal compliance. Platform API data is retained only for authorized brand operations and approved use cases.

Etsy Open API Data

The internal Etsy integration requests only listings_r, listings_w and shops_r. It processes shop and listing metadata for the owner's NumberNinjaDesigns shop and does not request buyer, address, payment, profile or transaction data.

Data Security

NumberNinjaDesigns applies least privilege and access limitation. Etsy API credentials and OAuth tokens are stored only on the private server, encrypted at rest, excluded from browser storage and logs, and removed when the connection is disconnected or no longer required. No public website feature asks for platform passwords.

Children's Privacy

This website and related internal brand tools are not directed to children and do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

International Transfers

Because external platforms may operate internationally, data handled through official APIs may be processed according to those providers' regional infrastructure and policies.

User Rights

Users may request access, correction or deletion by email. External platform account data should also be managed through the relevant platform's own privacy tools.

Contact for Privacy Requests

Email privacy@ninjanumbertees.com with a clear description of the request and the platform or communication involved.
